{# Grid/List for a trade's cards. On the PICKER screens this must NOT reload the page: the in-progress selection lives in trade-picker.js, and a round-trip would take it with it — the whole hazard #184 was about. So the toggle posts the preference with HTMX (`hx-swap="none"`) and then asks each pane to re-fetch itself, which re-renders the cards in the new shape while the picks stay put. `hx-on::after-request` fires the panes' own `refresh` trigger; the panes declare that trigger next to their hx-get, so this file names no endpoint. On the trade DETAIL page there is nothing to lose, so it posts normally and the 303 brings you back (`pick_panes` false). #}
